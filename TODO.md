# HI Acceptor — delivery outcomes

- [ ] **Free, ungated, local-first product** — “make it completely free”; do not implement paid activation, account activation, Telegram/WhatsApp activation or messaging, subscription, unlocks, login, or a required online account. Settings and request-status information remain on-device; do not send screen text to a server.
- [ ] **Ride-filter settings** — Provide configurable minimum fare, maximum fare, maximum pickup time, region/app selection, excluded destination terms, and preferred-location terms. Seed the video examples (12–80 SAR, 5 minutes, `Airport`, `مطار`, `hamara`) while allowing changes. Treat preferred terms as an annotation only; they do not override the configured hard filters.
- [ ] **Automatic acceptance for the selected driver apps** — On Android, monitor only selected supported ride-driver apps (Jeeny Driver `me.com.easytaxista` and Bolt Driver `ee.mtakso.driver`). Accept only when fare is inclusively within the configured bounds, pickup time is at or below the configured maximum, no excluded term occurs, an explicit accept control is identified, and all required fields are unambiguous. Fail closed otherwise and prevent duplicate taps for the same request.
- [ ] **Transparent, user-controlled Android permission and operation** — Explain the Accessibility access in-app, provide a route to Android Accessibility settings, and require the user to enable it. Keep automation off until explicitly enabled by the user. Do not tell the user to disable Play Protect or other Android security protections.
- [ ] **Status, local activity, and notifications** — Show active/inactive/permission status, explain why requests are accepted or skipped when data is available, and provide an optional local notification after acceptance. Keep activity local and permit clearing it.
- [ ] **Runnable Android app with native brand assets and simulator** — Keep the web preview labeled as a rule simulator (not a native integration); create launcher/adaptive icons and validate the rule module. Document that native Accessibility must be tested in an Android build/device; do not claim an APK build unless it actually completes.
